# Tax-aware actions (advisor v3 Phase 1) — design

_2026-11-17. Roadmap: [2026-11-16-advisor-v3-roadmap.md](../plans/2026-11-16-advisor-v3-roadmap.md) §3 Phase 1. Operator answers (11-17): wait cap 60 trading days; undated lots = short-term, flagged; loss harvesting as a reported line; surface on move cards + morning brief + Telegram digest._

## Intent

The verdict stays exactly what v1 says. What changes is the **action** attached to a SELL-grade verdict: *when* to sell (now, or after the 12-month line when the tax saved beats the measured cost of waiting) and *what it costs after tax*. The advisor stops being blind to the one thing that is certain about every sale — STT, impact and capital-gains tax — while making no new forecast.

## What was found while designing (changes the shape)

- Kanaklata's realised P&L this FY (2026-04-01 → 11-12) is **−₹47,479**; last FY −₹58,325 (`pnl_statements`). Short-term losses offset STCG and LTCG (s.70) and carry forward. So the tax on a sale is a **marginal FY number**, not a per-sale rate: today most gains are shielded, and "wait for LTCG" rarely pays until the shield is used up. The model must be marginal or it will lie.
- SELL-grade names fall on average **−4.6% in 20 trading days, −5.6% in 40** (call_outcomes, n=22–33). That is the empirical cost of waiting, and it is already measured daily — use it, don't assume.
- 11 of 23 Kanaklata holdings have lots with no buy date (tradebook starts 2026-02-11); Natwar has one day of fills. Treated as short-term and flagged until the Console tradebook lands.

## Goals / non-goals / anti-goals

- **Goals:** every SELL-grade row carries `indicators.tax` = {action, timing, tax now vs after, cost of waiting, lot status, harvest note}; every row carries its LTCG date; the brief and digest say it in one line; a replay over the P-60 SELL calls reports what the rule would have changed.
- **Non-goals:** no change to `verdict`, score, thresholds, rotation target or sizing (guarded by test); no new sells generated by harvesting; no Natwar-specific rules.
- **Anti-goals:** advising a wait on a name the data says keeps falling; reporting a tax figure that ignores the FY shield; silent assumptions about undated lots.

## Rules (the "how", decided)

**Inputs per row:** `symbol, quantity, average_price, last_price, verdict`, FIFO lots (`costs.tax_lots` over `tradebook` for that account), FY context `{ytd_net_realised_inr, ltcg_used_fy, fy_known}` (from `pnl_statements`: the period starting this FY's Apr 1; absent → 0 + `fy_known=False`), the empirical cost curve, `today`, `adv_inr` (20-day mean of close×volume from `candles`).

**Lots:** matched FIFO lots as known; `unmatched_qty` shares are a synthetic lot at `average_price` dated `None` → short-term, no LTCG date, `lots = 'partial'|'unknown'`.

**Marginal FY tax** `fy_tax(stcg, ltcg, ctx)`: losses first — `net = ytd_net_realised + stcg + ltcg`; tax is computed on the FY totals with and without this sale (`costs.STCG_PCT`, `LTCG_PCT`, `LTCG_EXEMPT_INR − ltcg_used_fy`), STCL offsetting both buckets; `tax_inr = tax(with) − tax(without)`, never negative. (Deliberate simplification, documented: brought-forward losses from earlier FYs are **not** counted — needs the ITR, not the broker statement.)

**Cost of waiting** `expected_drop_pct(days)`: from `call_outcomes`, source `advice`, SELL-grade verdicts, `mean(ret_pct)` at the nearest checkpoint ≤ days (5/10/20/40/60), linearly interpolated, floored at 0; `n < 20` at that checkpoint → `None` → no wait advice ("not enough history"). Refreshed with the Sunday lessons job into `app_config.tax_cost_curve` and read at 09:45.

**Action** for `verdict ∈ {SELL, SELL_ON_BOUNCE, TRIM}` on the sell quantity (`rotation_sell_qty` if set, else TRIM = half, else all):
1. `tax_now` = marginal tax selling today; `ltcg_date` = `costs.after_tax_pct(...)['ltcg_date']` of the earliest short-term lot that would become long-term; `wait_days` = trading days from today to it.
2. If `wait_days ≤ 60` and curve known: `tax_after` = marginal tax if sold on `ltcg_date` at today's price; `saving = tax_now − tax_after`; `wait_cost = sell_value × expected_drop_pct(wait_days)/100`.
3. `saving > wait_cost` → `action = 'sell after <ltcg_date>'`; else `'sell now'`. HOLD rows get `action = None`, LTCG date only.
4. Harvest note when `last_price < average_price`: `ytd_net ≥ 0` → "realising −₹L offsets ₹min(L, ytd) of this year's gains"; else "adds to this FY's ₹|ytd| net loss (carries forward 8 years)".
5. `text`: one sentence, e.g. `after tax: sell now · tax ₹0 (FY loss shield ₹47,479) · cost ₹1,240`, or `after tax: sell after 2027-01-07 (31 days) · saves ₹4,500 vs ₹2,100 expected drift · lots: 60 of 100 dated`.

**Surfaces:** `indicators.tax` on `portfolio_advice` and Natwar `account_calls` rows (JSON, no schema change); move-card chip + a line in the detail sheet; brief `kind: 'tax'` — "Tax: ATGL sell after 2027-01-07 (saves ₹4.5k); 3 names cross 12 months by Dec 15: …"; digest `🧾` line under each actionable name.

**Replay** `scripts/tax_replay.py`: every official SELL-grade call since 2026-08-25 → what the rule would have said then (lots as known today) → after-tax outcome at the chosen date vs selling on the call date, using `call_outcomes` paths. Reports: calls told to wait, tax saved, drift paid, net ₹ and %; written to `docs/reference/TAX_REPLAY.md`. **Gate for keeping the rule on:** net ≥ 0 on this window and re-read monthly (VERIFY row).

## Constraints

- Verdict/score/rotation/sizing byte-identical: a test runs `run_advisor`'s row assembly with and without the tax pass and diffs every key except `indicators.tax` and `reasons`.
- All three tax constants from `costs.py`; verify against the Finance Act before 2027-04-01 (brief chore line when FY changes).
- Pure logic in `tax_actions.py`; DB reads only in a thin `context_for(account, today)`.
- Nothing orders; the digest line is text.
